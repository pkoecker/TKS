S10 E PRO COMPACT – high storage capacity for high energy demand

The S10 E PRO COMPACT is designed for homes and smaller commercial applications with high annual energy demand, for example due to heat pumps, electric vehicles or large PV systems.

  • Up to 29.2 kWh of usable battery capacity with 7.5 to 9 kW charging and discharging power.
  • Storage capacity can be expanded to up to 52.3 kWh.
  • Up to 20 kWp recommended PV (Photovoltaic) capacity with two independent MPPTs (Maximum Power Point Trackers).
  • Up to 12 kW AC (Alternating Current) nominal output and 13.5 kVA maximum apparent power.
  • Two separate battery circuits provide high storage reserves and can maintain a permanently configured backup reserve.
  • Three-phase, solar-rechargeable backup power, EEBus, Modbus TCP, KNX, CAN-I/O and preparation for Vehicle2Home.

A special feature of the PRO series: two separate battery sets can, depending on the configuration, maintain a permanently available backup reserve.
